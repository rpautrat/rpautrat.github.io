Rémi Pautrat

I am a PhD student in the Computer Vision and Geometry (CVG) group at ETH Zurich under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Marc Pollefeys.

Prior joining the CVG, I did my bachelor at Ecole polytechnique in Paris and my master in Computer Science at ETH Zurich. I also collaborated with and interned at Meta Reality Labs, Leica Geosystems, INRIA Nancy - Grand Est, and MetraLabs.

Research

I am interested in Computer Vision, Robotics and Machine Learning in general. My research focuses on local feature detection and description based on deep learning, with applications (among others) to visual localization and 3D reconstruction. I am also quite interested in disentangling the factors of variation of an image: geometry, style, semantics, etc.
